Dog Grooming Frequency Guide

Proper grooming is essential for a dog’s health, comfort, and overall
well-being. Grooming needs vary depending on a dog’s size, coat type,
and lifestyle. Below is a general guide to grooming frequency based on
size categories.

Small Dogs (under 20 pounds) Small dogs such as Shih Tzus, Maltese, and
Pugs often require frequent grooming, especially those with longer
coats.

Brushing: 3–5 times per week (daily for long-haired breeds) Bathing:
Every 3–4 weeks Nail trimming: Every 3–4 weeks Ear cleaning: Weekly
check, clean as needed Notes: Small dogs are more prone to dental
issues, so regular teeth brushing (several times a week) is highly
recommended.

Medium Dogs (20–50 pounds) Medium-sized dogs like Bulldogs, Border
Collies, and Australian Shepherds have varied grooming needs depending
on coat type.

Brushing: 2–4 times per week Bathing: Every 4–6 weeks Nail trimming:
Every 3–4 weeks Ear cleaning: Weekly check, clean as needed Notes:
Double-coated breeds may require more frequent brushing during shedding
seasons to prevent matting and excess fur buildup.

Large Dogs (over 50 pounds) Large breeds such as Golden Retrievers,
German Shepherds, and Huskies often have thick or double coats that
require consistent maintenance.

Brushing: 3–5 times per week (daily during shedding seasons) Bathing:
Every 6–8 weeks Nail trimming: Every 3–4 weeks Ear cleaning: Weekly
check, clean as needed Notes: Regular grooming helps manage shedding and
keeps the coat healthy. Larger dogs may require more time and space for
grooming sessions.

General Considerations All dogs benefit from routine grooming schedules.
Frequency may increase based on activity level, outdoor exposure, and
health conditions. Dogs that spend more time outdoors may need more
frequent baths.

Additionally, professional grooming every 6–8 weeks can help maintain
coat health, especially for breeds with complex grooming needs.

In summary, grooming frequency varies by size and coat type, but regular
brushing, bathing, and maintenance are essential to keep any dog clean,
comfortable, and healthy.
